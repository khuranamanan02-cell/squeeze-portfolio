The Founder

Vikrant brings years of professional F&B and catering experience to Squeeze. He is a known name in Mohali's catering circuit and has served large-format crowds at the PCA Stadium, Mohali, during IPL and international cricket fixtures.

That operational discipline now sits behind a small campus counter — where he personally oversees quality, hygiene and consistency, every single day.
